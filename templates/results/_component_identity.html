{% comment %}What a component *is*: the catalog match, or the boxes to describe a new part. Shared by the submitter's listing-details form and the reviewer's tie editor, because it is the same job from two seats and the fields behind it come from one mixin. A second copy is how the two drift into offering different controls over the same data. Two shapes, mirroring the machine's identity card: - **Matched** - suggest the match and keep the boxes collapsed behind an override. Two prefilled text boxes over an entry the catalog already holds read as an invitation to retype it, and one stray character mints "Intel Core i3-10100T " beside the real thing. - **New, or already corrected** - the boxes, because describing the part is the whole point there, and a row somebody already corrected stays open so they can keep editing or undo it by retyping what the report said. - **Somebody who is not deciding** - a submitter. They get one question, behind a disclosure, and only if their machine reported something else worth offering. They pick a value, never a field: being asked which field holds the real model is being asked to understand the payload. Requires `entry` and `counter` (unique per row, for the ids). {% endcomment %} {% if entry.suggest_field %} {% comment %}Closed by default.
