What anonymous means here

A run listed anonymously shows Anonymous wherever a submitter would normally be named - the run page and the public API included.

It is not an anonymous submission. Reviewers and administrators still see your account, because they have to know whose evidence they are judging, and the run is still yours on your dashboard.

Any single run can be attributed or anonymized from its own page at any time, including after it is published - your runs are listed on your dashboard. At run time, --anonymous does the same thing for one run.
